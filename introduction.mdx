Bountiful Fares is a Minecraft mod for players who want more depth from the food and farming side of the game without leaving vanilla's design philosophy behind. It introduces fruit trees, new crop types, a fermentation system, dyeable ceramic blocks, trellis structures, and food effects — all crafted to feel like natural extensions of base Minecraft rather than a total overhaul.

## Key feature categories

Bountiful Fares is organized around six areas of content:

- **Fruit trees** — Grow apple, orange, lemon, plum, and coconut trees that generate naturally in the world and can be cultivated on your farm. Ancient variants like Hoary Apple Trees can be found via Sniffers.
- **New crops** — Discover and cultivate passion fruit, leeks, maize, spongekins, lapisberries, chamomile, and more. Wild variants generate across biomes.
- **Fermentation** — Use fermentation vessels to brew mead, cider, elderberry wine, pickled vegetables, and other fermented foods over time.
- **Ceramic blocks** — Craft ceramic clay from feldspar and clay balls, then shape it into tiles, slabs, stairs, and walls that can be dyed any of the 16 vanilla colors.
- **Trellises** — Build trellis structures for growing vine crops like tomatoes or displaying decorative plants. Fully data-driven and extensible via datapacks.
- **Food effects** — New status effects add meaningful decisions to what you eat. The Enrichment effect and the Acidify mechanic reward thoughtful food choices.

## Mod info

| Field               | Value                                          |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Current version     | 3.0.8                                          |
| Minecraft version   | 1.21.1                                         |
| Supported loaders   | Fabric, NeoForge                               |
| Required dependency | [NexusLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/nexuslib)  |

<Note>
  Bountiful Fares requires **NexusLib** to run. Download it from [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/nexuslib) and place the JAR in your mods folder alongside Bountiful Fares.
</Note>

<Note>
  Bountiful Fares is an **independent mod** — it is not a Farmer's Delight addon, though it does include optional compatibility with Farmer's Delight and several other mods. Its goal is to feel like a natural extension of vanilla Minecraft.
</Note>
