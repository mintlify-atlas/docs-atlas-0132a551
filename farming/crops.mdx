Bountiful Fares adds a range of new crops to the game, from everyday farmland staples to exotic ocean plants and ancient species recovered by the Sniffer. Some generate as wild variants in the world, while others require seeds or specific growing conditions.

## Farmland crops

These crops are planted on farmland (or compatible soil) using their respective seed items.

| Crop | Seed item | Nutrition | Saturation modifier | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leek | Leek Seeds | 3 | 0.6 | Standard farmland crop |
| Maize | Maize Seeds | 3 | 0.6 | Also grows on Rich Soil (Farmer's Delight) |
| Scorchkin | Scorchkin Seeds | — | — | Nether crop; seeds planted on soul sand |

<Note>
Maize can be processed into Popped Maize (nutrition 3, saturation 0.5), Maize Bread (nutrition 5, saturation 0.5), and several stuffed potato dishes.
</Note>

## Wild crops

Several crops generate naturally as wild blocks in the world, where you can harvest them without needing seeds. Breaking wild crops typically drops the produce directly. Wild variants for some vanilla crops are also added.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Passion Fruit">
    A fast-eating wild crop (nutrition 2, saturation modifier 0.1) found growing as a wild vine in warm biomes. Can also be planted on a trellis using the fruit itself as the "seed" — see [Trellises](/farming/trellises) for details. Wild Passion Fruit Vines generate in the world.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Elderberries">
    Wild berries (nutrition 2, saturation modifier 0.1) that have a **30% chance to instantly restore 1 health point** when eaten. Wild Elderberry Vines generate in the world. Can also be grown on a trellis.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wild vanilla crops">
    Wild Wheat, Wild Carrots, Wild Potatoes, Wild Beetroots, Wild Leeks, and Wild Maize all generate as natural blocks in applicable biomes, providing a way to find crops without raiding villages.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Ocean crops: Spongekin

Spongekin is an unusual crop that grows on **Sea Lanterns** inside Ocean Monuments.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Spongekin Sprouts">
    **Spongekin Sprouts** generate naturally on the floor of sponge rooms inside Ocean Monuments. Break them to obtain Spongekin Seeds (or use Shears or Silk Touch to collect the sprout itself).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grow Spongekin">
    Plant Spongekin Seeds on a Sea Lantern. The stem will grow upward over several stages, eventually producing a full Spongekin block. The advancement "Maribiogeological" tracks growing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Harvest">
    Break the Spongekin block to collect it. Spongekin can be crafted into Spongekin Slices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Spongekin Slice** (nutrition 2, saturation modifier 0.3, always edible) extends your air supply timer underwater for a short duration. **Pickled Spongekin** (nutrition 5, saturation modifier 0.8) provides a longer extension.

<Warning>
As of version 3.0.0, Guardians no longer drop Spongekin Seeds. You must find Spongekin Sprouts growing in Ocean Monument sponge rooms instead.
</Warning>

## Ancient crops: Lapisberries

Lapisberries are an ancient plant that can only be obtained through the **Sniffer**.

- The Sniffer can excavate **Lapisberry Seeds** from ancient dirt.
- Lapisberry Seeds are planted on farmland to grow a Lapisberry crop.
- **Lapisberries** (nutrition 3, saturation modifier 0.1) can be eaten directly or used to craft **Blue Dye** — making them a renewable source of blue dye.
- Lapisberries can also be grown on a trellis using Lapisberry Seeds.

## Tea plants

Tea plants provide ingredients for brewing several varieties of tea.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Tea Shrub" icon="leaf" href="/farming/crops">
    Grows naturally in the world. Right-click to harvest Tea Berries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tea Berries" icon="circle-dot" href="/farming/crops">
    Harvested from Tea Shrubs. Planting Tea Berries places a new Tea Shrub. Used to brew tea alongside Tea Leaves.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tea Leaves" icon="leaf" href="/farming/crops">
    Obtained by breaking Tea Shrub blocks. Dried into Dried Tea Leaves for use in tea recipes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Chamomile Flowers" icon="sun" href="/farming/crops">
    Grown on farmland. When Bone Meal is applied on farmland, extra flowers drop as items instead of cycling back to 1 flower.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Tea cups (green, black, chamomile, honeysuckle, bellflower, torchflower) each restore 4 nutrition with a saturation modifier of 1.0 and grant the **Ebullience** effect for 5 minutes.

## Utility seeds

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sweet Berry Pips" icon="seedling" href="/farming/crops">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Grass Seeds" icon="wheat" href="/datapacks/grass-seeds">
