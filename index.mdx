Bountiful Fares is a Minecraft mod for Fabric and NeoForge that expands the food and farming side of the game with dozens of new items, blocks, and mechanics. From growing coconut palms and citrus trees to brewing mead in fermentation vessels, the mod adds depth without straying from Minecraft's design philosophy.

## Key features

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fruit trees">
    Plant and harvest apples, oranges, lemons, plums, and coconuts from dedicated fruit trees that generate naturally and can be cultivated on your farm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="New crops & wild plants">
    Discover passion fruit, leeks, maize, spongekins, and more. Wild variants generate in biomes and can be cultivated on farmland or trellises.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fermentation & brewing">
    Fill a fermentation vessel with fruit juices or honey to produce mead, cider, elderberry wine, and lapisberry wine over time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ceramic building blocks">
    Craft ceramic clay from feldspar and clay balls, then fire it into tiles, slabs, stairs, and walls that can be dyed any of the 16 vanilla colors.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Bountiful Fares currently supports **Minecraft 1.21.1** on both Fabric and NeoForge. It requires [NexusLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/nexuslib) as a dependency.
</Note>
