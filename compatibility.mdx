---
title: "Compatible mods and integration features"
description: "Bountiful Fares auto-detects compatible mods and activates integrations for trellis types, food items, recipe display, and UI improvements — no config needed."
---

Bountiful Fares detects compatible mods at startup and activates the relevant integration automatically — no configuration required. Compatibility content appears in a dedicated creative tab when the corresponding mod is present. Mods that are only available on a specific loader are labelled accordingly.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Nature's Spirit" icon="tree">
    Adds trellis and picket variants for all Nature's Spirit wood types: Aspen, Cedar, Coconut, Cypress, Fir, Ghaf, Joshua, Larch, Mahogany, Maple, Olive, Palo Verde, Sugi, Willow, and Wisteria.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Amendments" icon="droplets">
    Bountiful Fares items can be used with Amendments cauldron fluids. Unique mod cakes play the correct cake eating sounds, including Amendments' Double Cake and directional Cake.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Arts and Crafts" icon="hammer">
    Adds Cork Trellis and Cork Pickets, crafted from Arts and Crafts cork planks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Appledog" icon="bone">
    Adds the Appledog Block — a decorative block crafted from nine Dogapples.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Man's Land (NeoForge)" icon="snowflake">
    Adds Pine, Maple, Walnut, and Willow trellises and pickets. Also adds Candied Pears and Maple Mead Bottles — brewed by fermenting Maple Syrup Bottles in a fermentation vessel. Maple Mead removes the Hunger, Weakness, and Slowness effects when consumed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Frontiers (Fabric)" icon="spa">
    Adds trellis plants for Ancient Rose Bush and Violet Rose Bush. Also adds Eboncork Trellises and Eboncork Pickets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Farmer's Delight" icon="wheat">
    Maize can be planted on Rich Soil Farmland. Chamomile and other tea flowers can be bonemealed on and by Rich Soil Farmland. Tomatoes can be planted on Trellises.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dungeon's Delight" icon="skull">
    Adds Wormwood Trellis and Wormwood Pickets, plus the Stained Scrap Railing block. Added in version 3.0.8.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AppSkin (Fabric)" icon="utensils">
    Food values of items stored inside Shulker Tiffins are displayed correctly in the tooltip overlay.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mod Menu (Fabric)" icon="sliders">
    Bountiful Fares mod settings are accessible through the Mod Menu interface.
  </Card>
  <Card title="JEI" icon="book-open">
    Recipe display support for all Bountiful Fares custom recipe types, including fermentation, milling, propagation, and ceramic mass-dyeing.
  </Card>
  <Card title="EMI" icon="book-open">
    Recipe display support for all Bountiful Fares custom recipe types, including fermentation, milling, propagation, and ceramic mass-dyeing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  A separate creative tab is created for compatibility content. Items from a given integration only appear there when the corresponding mod is loaded.
</Note>

<Note>
  **No Man's Land** integration is available on NeoForge only. **Frontiers** and **AppSkin** integrations are available on Fabric only.
</Note>
