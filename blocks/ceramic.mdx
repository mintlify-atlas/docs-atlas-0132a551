Bountiful Fares adds a complete set of ceramic building and decoration blocks, all sharing a unified dyeing system. The material chain begins with **Feldspar**, a new ore-like material item, and **Clay Balls**. Combine them in a crafting table to produce **Ceramic Clay**, which is then fired or shaped into **Ceramic Tiles** — the foundation for every ceramic block form in the mod.

## Obtaining ceramic materials

<Steps>
  <Step title="Gather Feldspar">
    Feldspar is a new material item added by Bountiful Fares. It is the key ingredient that distinguishes ceramic clay from ordinary terracotta.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Craft Ceramic Clay">
    Combine Feldspar with Clay Balls in a crafting table to produce Ceramic Clay. Crafting Ceramic Clay unlocks the **"It's Tofu!!!"** advancement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape into building blocks">
    Ceramic Clay is processed into Ceramic Tiles, which can then be crafted or stonecuttered into every available block form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ceramic Tiles come in a full range of building shapes, including structural and interactive variants.

## Dyeing ceramic blocks

All ceramic blocks share the same dyeing system. A block's color is stored in its block entity and is preserved when the block is picked up.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Dye directly">
    Right-click any ceramic block with a vanilla dye item to apply that color. All 16 vanilla dye colors are supported.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use the Artisan Brush">
    The **Artisan Brush** is a craftable tool dedicated to dyeing. Right-click a ceramic block with a loaded brush to apply its color. Right-clicking a dye item onto the brush in your inventory loads the brush with that color.

    **Inventory dyeing:** Right-click the brush over a stack of dyeable items — leather armor, wolf armor, or ceramic blocks — in an inventory to dye the entire stack at once.

    **Armor Stand dyeing:** Use a dyed Artisan Brush on an Armor Stand that has leather armor equipped to dye the piece directly on the stand.

    Crafting the Artisan Brush unlocks the **"I'm Something of an Artist"** advancement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reset color">
    Right-click any dyed ceramic block with a **Wet Sponge** (without sneaking) to revert it to its default uncolored state.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy color from block">
    Right-clicking a dyed block with an undyed Artisan Brush picks up that block's color onto the brush, ready to apply elsewhere.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Dyeing any ceramic block any color unlocks the **"Roy G Biv"** advancement. Dyeing a piece of leather armor on an Armor Stand unlocks **"High Fashion"**.
</Tip>

### Checkered mosaic pattern

If a ceramic tile is already dyed and you apply the same color again using the Artisan Brush, the block converts to its checkered mosaic variant. Applying a Wet Sponge to a checkered block reverts it to the standard tile form.

### Map color

Since 3.0.0, dyeable ceramic blocks show as the closest available vanilla map color when they appear on a filled Map.

## Solid Ceramic

**Solid Ceramic** is a special command-only variant of the ceramic tile. It behaves like all other dyeable ceramic blocks but has a solid, flat-color texture rather than the tiled pattern.

<Note>
  Solid Ceramic cannot be obtained in Survival mode — it must be given with `/give`. It is intended for builders and map-makers who want true solid color panels.
</Note>

Using a **Glow Ink Sac** on a placed Solid Ceramic block enables its **LIT** state, making the surface emit a true glowing solid color with no texture detail visible.

## Tinged Glass

**Tinged Glass** is a dyeable colored glass variant added by the mod. It extends vanilla's `TransparentBlock` and reduces light passage to one-fifth of the maximum level (`getLightBlock` returns `maxLightLevel / 5`). Like other ceramic blocks it can be dyed in all 16 colors.

## Creative tab placement

Since 3.0.0, ceramic building blocks are also added to the vanilla **Colored Blocks** creative inventory tab alongside wool, concrete, and stained glass.
