Bountiful Fares expands Minecraft's food system with dozens of new items spread across fruits, crops, prepared meals, baked goods, bottled drinks, and fermented products. Most items carry nutrition and saturation values tuned to reward thoughtful foraging and cooking, and several grant custom mob effects that give lasting combat and exploration benefits.

## The Shulker Tiffin

The Shulker Tiffin is a portable food container that lets you carry large quantities of a single food item without worrying about stack-size limits.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Crafting">
    Craft a Shulker Tiffin with **2 Shulker Shells** and a **Bowl** in a crafting table. Tiffins can be dyed any of the 16 dye colors using an Artisan Brush or by replacing the Bowl with a dye in the recipe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Storing food">
    Right-click a Tiffin with a food item to fill it. A single Tiffin holds up to **64 of any one food item**, regardless of that item's normal stack size. Foods with container items (like bowl meals or bottled drinks) will leave their container behind in the Tiffin as you eat, and you need a stack of that container in your inventory to retrieve the food.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Eating from the Tiffin">
    You can eat food directly from the Tiffin by right-clicking while holding it — no need to take items out first. Any crafting recipe that uses a food's container can substitute a Tiffin in that slot, crafting the item directly into the Tiffin.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Compatibility">
    The Appleskin mod will correctly display food values for items stored inside a Tiffin when hovering over it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Fill a Tiffin with Mead Bottles or compote jars before heading into a dungeon — you can eat directly from it and it holds far more than a normal inventory slot allows.
</Tip>
