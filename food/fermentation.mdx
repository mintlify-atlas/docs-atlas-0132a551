The Fermentation Vessel is a craftable block that converts raw ingredients into fermented products over time. Mead, fruit wines, ciders, pickled vegetables, and other items can only be obtained through fermentation, making the vessel an essential part of advanced food production. The vessel also outputs a redstone comparator signal, enabling fully automated brewing farms.

## How the Fermentation Vessel works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill with water">
    Right-click the empty Fermentation Vessel with a water source to fill it. Accepted water sources include a **Water Bucket** (returns an empty Bucket), a **Water Bottle** (returns a Glass Bottle), or a **Water Cup** (returns a Cup). The vessel enters the `WATER` state.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the ingredient">
    Right-click the water-filled vessel with a valid fermentation ingredient. The ingredient is consumed and fermentation begins. If the ingredient has a crafting remainder (such as a Glass Bottle), it is returned to your inventory. The vessel enters the `FERMENTING` state and colored bubble particles appear above it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for fermentation to complete">
    The vessel ticks over time until the product is ready. A **comparator placed against the vessel** reads 1–15 based on fermentation progress; the signal reaches **15 when fermentation is complete**. You can monitor progress without opening any inventory screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect the product">
    Right-click the vessel once fermentation is done to retrieve the finished product. The vessel returns to the `EMPTY` state, ready for a new batch.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Place a comparator on the side of the Fermentation Vessel and wire it into a note block or indicator lamp. When the signal reaches 15, your brew is ready to collect.
</Tip>
